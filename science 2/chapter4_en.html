Meeting present needs while conserving resources for the future is sustainable development.

  • c) Run, Play, Sleep
  • d) Sell, Buy, Profit

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle is the principle of environmental protection.

  • a) Factories
  • b) Sanctuaries and National Parks
  • c) Agriculture
  • d) Settlements
✅ Answer: b) Sanctuaries and National Parks

Wildlife conservation is done through sanctuaries, national parks, and tiger reserves.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Pollution – Environmental protection

Pollution harms the environment; protection means preventing it.

✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Biodiversity – Uniformity

Biodiversity means variety; uniformity is its opposite.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: The variety of different species of plants, animals, and microorganisms, their habitats, and ecosystems on Earth is biodiversity. It maintains ecosystem balance.
✅ Answer: Development that meets the needs of the present while ensuring that future generations have access to resources is sustainable development.
✅ Answer: Measures taken to preserve natural resources, wildlife, and the environment are called conservation. It includes tree planting, water conservation, and sanctuaries.
Explain with Reason
✅ Explanation: Biodiversity sustains food chains, pollination, and soil fertilization; loss of one species affects the entire ecosystem.
✅ Explanation: Recycling reduces waste, saves new raw materials, reduces pollution, and conserves resources; therefore it is essential.
Fill in the Blanks
✅ Answer: Abiotic

Soil, water, air, sunlight are abiotic components.

✅ Answer: 3 R

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle is the 3 R principle.

✅ Answer: Sanctuaries

Sanctuaries and national parks are protected areas.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: The environment has biotic (living) and abiotic (soil, water, air, sunlight) components. Biodiversity maintains ecosystem balance, provides food, medicines, clean air and water. Its degradation causes environmental crises.
✅ Answer: Conservation is achieved through tree planting, water conservation, sanctuaries, pollution control, and the 3 R principle. Sustainable development means meeting present needs while conserving resources for the future. This sustains both environment and economy.
✅ Answer: Forests conserve soil and water, maintain rainfall, and absorb CO2. Water conservation increases groundwater and prevents drought. Water conservation is done through watersheds, ponds, and trenches.
