Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Renewable sources (solar, wind, hydro, bio) are naturally replenished and are clean. Non-renewable sources (coal, petroleum, natural gas) deplete once used and take millions of years to form; they cause pollution.
✅ Answer: Solar energy: conversion of sunlight to electricity via solar panels. Wind energy: electricity generation by turbines using wind kinetic energy. Both are clean, sustainable, and used in rural areas.
✅ Answer: CO2, methane gases cause Earth's temperature rise (global warming), ice melts, sea levels rise. Remedies: Energy conservation, use of renewable sources, tree planting, pollution control.
✅ Answer: Green energy is environmentally friendly, clean, and sustainable. It provides energy independence, reduces pollution and global warming, and creates rural employment; hence it is essential for the future.
