Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Organisms are classified hierarchically into kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species. Whittaker's five kingdoms: Monera (unicellular prokaryotes), Protista (unicellular eukaryotes), Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.
✅ Answer: Chordata has a notochord. Subphyla: (1) Pisces – fins and scales, (2) Amphibia – both water and land, (3) Reptilia – scales, (4) Aves – wings and feathers, (5) Mammalia – mammary glands and hair. They show evolutionary sequence.
✅ Answer: Arthropods have an exoskeleton, jointed legs, body divided into cephalothorax and abdomen. Includes insects, shrimp, scorpions, crabs. It is the largest and most diverse phylum.
