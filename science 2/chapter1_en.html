✅ Answer: A sudden change in the position or sequence of a nucleotide in a gene is called mutation. It can occur naturally or due to radioactive or chemical factors.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Heredity is the process of transmitting biological traits from one generation to the next through genes. Genes are located in chromosomes and reach the next generation through chromosomes. When there is a sudden change in the sequence of nucleotides in genes, mutation occurs; also during reproduction, mixing of genes creates new combinations. Such hereditary changes create variation over generations.
✅ Answer: mRNA is produced according to the gene sequence on DNA (Transcription). mRNA comes from the nucleus to the cytoplasm. There, on ribosomes, tRNA brings appropriate amino acids and a protein chain is formed (Translation). Each codon has a complementary anticodon. This entire process is called the Central Dogma.
✅ Answer: Main evidence of evolution: (1) Morphological – similar traits found in plants and animals. (2) Anatomical – bone structure of hands, legs, wings, fins is similar. (3) Vestigial organs – non-functional remnants. (4) Paleontological/Fossils – underground remains. (5) Connecting links – Peripatus, Duck-billed Platypus. (6) Embryological – embryos of vertebrates are similar.
✅ Answer: The human lineage began about 70 million years ago. After dinosaurs disappeared, ape-like animals evolved from small mammals. About 25 million years ago, chimpanzees and gorillas diverged. About 20 million years ago, some ape species progressed in a different direction. As forests disappeared, apes came down, stood upright, and hand use increased. The sequence was Homo habilis, Homo erectus, Homo sapiens. About 50,000 years ago, the wise human (Homo sapiens) emerged.
