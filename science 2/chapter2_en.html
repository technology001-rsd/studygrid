Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Nutrition is taking necessary food and processing it to obtain energy. Plants are autotrophs; they prepare their own food through photosynthesis. Animals and humans are heterotrophs; they need to take food from outside and digest it.
✅ Answer: The circulatory system includes the heart, blood vessels, and blood. The heart is a four-chambered pump. Arteries carry oxygenated blood to the body, veins bring deoxygenated blood to the heart, gas exchange occurs in capillaries. Blood contains plasma, red and white blood cells, and platelets. This is a double circulation system.
✅ Answer: Respiration is the oxidation of food with the help of oxygen to obtain energy in cells. External respiration – gas exchange in lungs; Internal respiration – energy production in cells. Types: aerobic and anaerobic.
✅ Answer: The process of removing nitrogenous waste products (urea, uric acid) and excess water produced from metabolism is called excretion. Kidneys, lungs, skin are excretory organs. Accumulation of waste causes poisoning; therefore excretion is essential.
