Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Cell has cell membrane (protection and exchange), nucleus (DNA, control), cytoplasm, mitochondria (energy), ribosomes (proteins), Golgi body, lysosomes (digestion). Plant cells have cell wall and chloroplasts. All tissues and organs are made of cells.
✅ Answer: The desired gene is extracted and inserted into a vector (plasmid) to create recombinant DNA (rDNA), which is then introduced into bacteria/yeast. Products like insulin, interferon, growth hormones are obtained. This is called genetic engineering.
✅ Answer: Medicine (insulin, vaccines, antibiotics), Agriculture (biofertilizers, BT cotton, disease-resistant seeds), Environment (wastewater treatment, biopesticides), Food processing (yeast, enzymes). This improves health and economy.
