Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: According to WHO, health is complete physical, mental and social well-being. Social health means everyone in society is healthy. Hygiene, vaccination, nutrition, education keep society healthy and the nation progresses.
✅ Answer: TB, cholera, AIDS, malaria are infectious diseases. Prevention: hygiene, clean water, vaccination, mosquito control, safe sexual practices, proper antibiotic use. This prevents disease spread.
✅ Answer: Diabetes, hypertension, cancer, heart disease are non-infectious diseases. Causes: unhealthy lifestyle, malnutrition, smoking, genetics. Prevention: balanced diet, exercise, check-ups, avoiding smoking.
