Write a Note
✅ Answer: Unicellular, prokaryotic microorganisms. Shape-wise: Coccus (spherical), Bacillus (rod), Spirilla (spiral). They help in food production, medicine, decomposition; some cause diseases.
✅ Answer: Ultra-microscopic organisms. Non-living outside cells, reproduce inside cells. Cause diseases like Dengue, Influenza, AIDS. Antibiotics do not work on them.
✅ Answer: Medicines used for bacterial diseases. Penicillin was the first antibiotic. Overuse should be avoided as bacteria develop resistance.
Explain with Reason
✅ Explanation: Antibiotics work on bacterial metabolism; viruses lack their own metabolism and depend on host cells; therefore antibiotics are not effective on viruses.
✅ Explanation: Vaccines produce antibodies in the body; when the actual pathogen enters, it is quickly destroyed; hence disease does not occur.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: Microorganisms are microscopic organisms invisible to the naked eye. Types: Bacteria, Viruses, Fungi (yeast, mold), Protozoa, Algae. They are useful in soil enrichment, food, medicine; some cause diseases.
✅ Answer: Coccus (spherical), Bacillus (rod), Spirilla (spiral), Vibrio (comma shaped). Uses: curd/buttermilk production (lactic acid bacteria), nitrogen fixation, medicine, decomposition. Harm: TB, cholera, typhoid.
✅ Answer: Antibiotics are used for bacterial diseases (penicillin, tetracycline). In vaccination, a dead/weak part of the pathogen is given; it produces antibodies and develops immunity (measles, polio vaccine). Both are remedies for infectious diseases.
