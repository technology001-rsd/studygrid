Detailed Answers
✅ Answer: The excretory system consists of kidneys, ureters, urinary bladder, and urethra. Nephrons in the kidneys filter blood and excrete urea, water, and salts as urine. This maintains fluid and salt balance in the blood and removes toxic substances.
✅ Answer: Control is achieved by the Central Nervous System (brain and spinal cord) and the autonomic nervous system. Nerve cells send nerve impulses. The brain controls all activities, reflexes occur through the spinal cord. This provides coordination and protection to the body.
✅ Answer: Endocrine glands secrete hormones: Thyroid – Thyroxine (growth and metabolism), Pancreas – Insulin (sugar control), Adrenal – Adrenaline (stress response), Pituitary – Growth Hormone. These hormones maintain coordination in the body.
✅ Answer: Control means regulating body activities through the brain and nervous system. Coordination means adjusting the functions of various organs with each other. The nervous system performs fast (electrical) coordination, while hormones achieve slow and long-term coordination.
