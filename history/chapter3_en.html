Long Answers
The practical use of history in the present is applied history. Examples: preserving old settlements in urban planning, developing historical sites in tourism, exhibiting objects in museums, using historical climate records in environmental planning.
While developing cities, studying old buildings, roads, and water systems helps retain the historical character. Old wadas are preserved and harmonised with new development.
Historical forts, caves, and monuments are tourist attractions. Tourism preserves those places and boosts the local economy; people get direct acquaintance with history.
Local history tells people about their land and ancestors. It creates the village's identity, pride, and unity, and awareness to preserve heritage.
