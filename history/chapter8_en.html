Multiple Choice Questions

  • b) Historical

✅ Answer: b) Historical

The caves and sculptures at Ajanta-Ellora are world-class historical heritage sites.

  • a) Temples
  • b) Forts of Shivaji's era
  • c) Universities
  • d) Ports
✅ Answer: b) Forts of Shivaji's era

Rajgad and Raigad are important forts and historical sites from the time of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (3) Hampi – Trading port

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara empire and a place of historical buildings, not a trading port. Hence this pair is wrong.

Write a Note
Visiting historical sites, forts, caves, monuments is historical tourism. It lets one experience history directly and preserve heritage.
Places in UNESCO's world heritage list. Ajanta-Ellora, Ellora, Hampi, Taj Mahal are in India; preserving them is the nation's duty.
Explain Statements with Reasons
Visitor footfall leads to maintenance of sites, and government and locals strive to preserve; thus historical heritage survives.
Historical sites are tourist attractions; their development creates employment and improves the economy, yet heritage must be preserved.
Complete the Concept Map
Historical tourism (forts, caves) → Religious tourism (pilgrim centres) → Environmental tourism (sanctuaries) → Adventure tourism (trekking) → Cultural tourism (festivals).
Long Answers
Understanding history by visiting historical places is historical tourism. Ajanta-Ellora, Ellora, Hampi, Taj Mahal, Rajgad-Raigad, Sindhudurg are examples.
Ajanta-Ellora (world heritage), Ellora, Aurangabad caves, Rajgad, Raigad, Sindhudurg, Panhala, Shaniwar Wada tell the Shivaji-era and medieval history.
Tourism boosts hotels, transport, handicrafts, guiding; locals get employment. Historical sites as tourism centres improve the economy.
With more tourism, site maintenance, security, and publicity increase. Local society takes pride in heritage and becomes alert to preserve it.
