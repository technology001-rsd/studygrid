Newspapers built public opinion against British rule and took the freedom movement to the people. Kesari, Maratha, Young India, Harijan played important roles in national awakening.

Questions Based on Passage (Akashvani)
Akashvani (AIR) comes under the Government of India's 'Ministry of Information and Broadcasting'.
The British government renamed the 'Indian Broadcasting Company' (IBC) as the 'Indian State Broadcasting Service' (ISBS); later on 8 June 1936 it was named 'All India Radio' (AIR).
Doordarshan started programmes in 24 languages and 146 dialects; hence it is a multilingual medium.
On the suggestion of Pandit Narendra Sharma, the name 'Akashvani' was given. Special programmes for farmers, workers, students, and private radio are broadcast.
Complete the Concept Map
Newspapers (Kesari, Maratha, Young India) → Akashvani/Radio (AIR, 1936) → Doordarshan (1959) → Internet and social media (modern media). Through this chain, history is disseminated and preserved.
