Multiple Choice Questions
  • a) Voltaire
  • b) René Descartes
  • c) Leopold Ranke
  • d) Karl Marx
✅ Answer: a) Voltaire

Voltaire gave importance to a holistic consideration of society in historiography; hence he is regarded as the father of modern historiography.

Long Answers
Karl Marx presented the theory of 'historical materialism'. According to him, history is a class struggle. Ownership of the means of production and the resulting economic system is the fundamental driving force of history. He believed that socialism would follow capitalism.

Feminist historiography is the reconstruction of history from women's perspective. It studies women's economic, social, and political contributions and injustices, and presents their place in history.
Ranke's famous dictum 'wie es eigentlich gewesen' means 'write events as they actually happened'. He emphasized neutral, objective historiography based on primary sources.
