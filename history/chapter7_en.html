Long Answers
Olympics in ancient Greece was a tradition of sports and culture. Revived in 1896, modern Olympics is a symbol of friendship and competition in world history; nations' history is made through it.
Cricket came from England; took root in India during British rule. India started Test cricket in 1932. The 1983 and 2011 World Cup victories made Indian cricket history golden.
Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, Mallakhamb, wrestling, canambali are originally Indian. Mallakhamb and wrestling are famous in Maharashtra. These preserve physical and cultural heritage.
In major sports, the country is represented; victory brings joy and pride across the country. Sports unite the nation beyond language and province.
