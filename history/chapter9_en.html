Complete the Concept Map

Long Answers
Museums preserve historical objects and show them to the public through exhibitions. Heritage stays alive, students learn, and people take pride in history.
Archives preserve documents and records; libraries preserve books, manuscripts, and periodicals. Both keep written history's evidence and support research.
Forts, temples, wadas are maintained, rebuilt, and protected by ASI/state departments. Digital records and awareness through tourism help preserve built heritage.
When manuscripts, photographs, documents are stored digitally, they are saved from destruction. Online availability increases research and dissemination; this is a modern preservation method.
