✅ Answer: c) 4

Carbon has 4 electrons in its outer shell, so its valency is 4; it forms 4 covalent bonds.

  • a) High reactivity
  • b) Catenation
  • c) Electrical conductivity
  • d) Magnetism
✅ Answer: b) Catenation

Carbon can form long chains/rings with its own atoms, called catenation; this results in thousands of compounds.

  • a) C₂H₆
  • b) CH₄
  • c) C₃H₈
  • d) C₂H₄
✅ Answer: b) CH₄

Methane (CH₄) is the simplest hydrocarbon; it is saturated (single bonds).

  • a) C₂H₅OH
  • b) CH₃COOH
  • c) CH₃OH
  • d) C₆H₁₂O₆
✅ Answer: a) C₂H₅OH

Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) is an alcohol; it contains the –OH group.

  • a) –OH
  • b) –COOH
  • c) –CHO
  • d) –NH₂
✅ Answer: b) –COOH

The carboxyl group is –COOH; it is characteristic of carboxylic acids (e.g., acetic acid).

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Butane – C₄H₈

Butane is a saturated hydrocarbon with formula C₄H₁₀; C₄H₈ is butene (unsaturated).

✅ Wrong Pair: (3) Acetylene – C₂H₄

Acetylene (ethyne) has the formula C₂H₂; C₂H₄ is ethene (ethylene).

Write a Note
✅ Answer: A bond formed by sharing a pair of electrons between two atoms is called a covalent bond. Carbon compounds are formed by covalent bonds.
✅ Answer: The property of carbon to form long chains, branches, and rings with its own atoms is called catenation; this results in a vast number of carbon compounds.
✅ Answer: The –COOH group found in carboxylic acids gives them acidic properties. E.g., acetic acid (CH₃COOH).
✅ Answer: Hydrocarbons with only single bonds are saturated (e.g., methane), while those with double/triple bonds are unsaturated (e.g., ethene, acetylene).

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Carbon has a valency of 4 and can join with its own atoms to form chains/rings (catenation). This produces countless compounds (hydrocarbons, alcohols, acids, polymers). Carbon forms covalent bonds.
✅ Answer:
Saturated (alkanes): only C–C single bonds, formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (methane, ethane). Unsaturated: alkenes with double bonds (CₙH₂ₙ, ethene) and alkynes with triple bonds (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, acetylene). Unsaturated are more reactive.
✅ Answer:
Ethanol (C₂H₅OH): alcohol, used in beverages, medicines, solvents. Ethanoic/acetic acid (CH₃COOH): carboxyl group, vinegar, dyes, plastics (PVC) industry. Both undergo oxidation/esterification.
