Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Extracting and purifying metals from ores is metallurgy. Stages: (1) Mineral processing (froth flotation), (2) Reduction – extracting metal from oxide (coke/electrolysis), (3) Purification to obtain pure metal.
✅ Answer:
When electric current passes through a solution, positive and negative ions move to the cathode and anode respectively, and metal is deposited. Used for aluminum (electrolysis of Al₂O₃), sodium, potassium.
✅ Answer:
Alloys are harder, corrosion-resistant, and more durable than pure metals. Examples: Steel (Fe+C) – construction; Brass (Cu+Zn) – tools; Bronze (Cu+Sn) – statues; Stainless steel (Fe+Cr+Ni) – utensils.
