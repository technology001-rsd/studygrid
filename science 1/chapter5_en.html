✅ Answer: b) Calorimeter

A calorimeter measures the heat absorbed or released by an object.

  • a) Lowest
  • b) Highest
  • c) Medium
  • d) Zero
✅ Answer: b) Highest

The specific heat of water (4180 J/kg°C) is higher than most other substances; therefore it stores heat well.

  • a) Cold to hot
  • b) Hot to cold
  • c) Upward
  • d) Downward
✅ Answer: b) Hot to cold

Heat always flows from a hot object to a cold object (from higher temperature to lower temperature).

  • a) Heat
  • b) Coolness
  • c) Pressure
  • d) Light
✅ Answer: b) Coolness

During evaporation, surface molecules absorb heat and escape, so the surface becomes cool (coolness is felt).

  • a) Absorbs heat
  • b) Releases heat
  • c) Emits light
  • d) Generates electricity
✅ Answer: b) Releases heat

When a liquid changes to a solid (freezing), the substance releases heat; this is called the heat of crystallization.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Evaporation – Heat production

In evaporation, heat is absorbed (cooling occurs), not produced; therefore this pair is wrong.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: The amount of heat required to raise the temperature of 1 kg of a substance by 1°C is called its specific heat. For water, it is 4180 J/kg°C.
✅ Answer: The process of surface molecules of a liquid absorbing heat and escaping as vapor is called evaporation. This causes a cooling effect.
✅ Answer: Heat always flows from a higher temperature to a lower temperature (from hot to cold) until both objects reach the same temperature.
Explain the Statements with Reasons
✅ Answer:
Water absorbs heat slowly and releases it slowly; therefore it remains cooler than land during the day and warmer at night, maintaining a moderate climate in coastal areas.
✅ Answer:
During evaporation, surface molecules absorb the required heat and escape, reducing the temperature of the remaining surface, causing a feeling of coolness.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Specific heat is the heat required to raise the temperature of 1 kg of a substance by 1°C. Advantages of water's high specific heat: (1) Body temperature is regulated, (2) Coastal areas have moderate climate, (3) Used as coolant in engines, (4) Food takes longer to cook.
✅ Answer:
Factors affecting evaporation: (1) Surface area – larger area means more evaporation; (2) Temperature – higher temperature means more evaporation; (3) Wind speed; (4) Humidity in air – lower humidity means more evaporation.
✅ Answer:
Heat flows from hot to cold. In a calorimeter, water of known specific heat is taken and a hot object is placed in it; the heat given by the object is calculated from the temperature rise of water using the formula Q = mcΔT.
