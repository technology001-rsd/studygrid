✅ Answer: A program to send satellites, spacecraft, and probes into space beyond Earth's atmosphere is called a space mission; e.g., Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan.
✅ Answer: A natural or artificial object revolving in orbit around Earth is called a satellite. Artificial satellites are used for communication, weather, and observation.
✅ Answer: A rocket system that releases satellites and spacecraft from Earth's gravity into orbit is called a launch vehicle; e.g., PSLV, GSLV.
✅ Answer: The Indian Space Research Organisation is India's space research organization; it has successfully conducted missions like Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, and NavIC.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Chandrayaan-1 (2008): Discovered water on the Moon. Chandrayaan-2 (2019): Targeted the Moon's south pole. Mangalyaan (2014): Success in Mars orbit, first attempt. NavIC (2013): Navigation satellites. These strengthened India's position in the space sector.
✅ Answer:
Satellites are essential for communication, weather forecasting, navigation, defense, and Earth observation. Launch vehicles (PSLV/GSLV) place them in orbit; indigenous launch vehicles provide self-reliance and low cost.
✅ Answer:
Space research provides: (1) Telecommunications and internet, (2) Accurate weather and natural disaster warnings, (3) GPS/navigation, (4) Earth resource and environmental monitoring, (5) Scientific knowledge and technological progress.
