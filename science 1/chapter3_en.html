Lavoisier proposed the law of conservation of mass: 'In a chemical reaction, the total mass does not change.'

  • a) Combination
  • b) Decomposition
  • c) Displacement
  • d) Double displacement
✅ Answer: a) Combination

When two or more substances combine to form a new substance, it is a combination reaction. Mg and O₂ combine to form MgO.

  • a) Combination
  • b) Decomposition
  • c) Displacement
  • d) Double displacement
✅ Answer: c) Displacement

In a displacement reaction, a more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its salt.

  • a) Endothermic
  • b) Exothermic
  • c) Decomposition
  • d) Inductive
✅ Answer: b) Exothermic

In an exothermic reaction, heat is produced; in an endothermic reaction, heat is absorbed.

  • a) Atomic number
  • b) Coefficients
  • c) Atomic mass
  • d) Groups
✅ Answer: b) Coefficients

By placing appropriate coefficients (numbers) in front of the components in the equation, the masses of reactants and products are made equal; this is called balancing.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (3) Displacement – AgNO₃+NaCl→AgCl+NaNO₃

AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃ is a double displacement reaction, not simple displacement; Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu is a simple displacement.

✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Rutherford – Periodic table

Rutherford discovered the structure of the atom (nucleus); the periodic table is the work of Mendeleev and Moseley.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: In a chemical reaction, the total mass of reactants remains equal to the total mass of products; i.e., mass is neither created nor destroyed.
✅ Answer: When two or more simple substances combine to form a new compound, a combination reaction occurs; e.g., 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.
✅ Answer: When a compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances with the help of heat, electricity, or light, a decomposition reaction occurs; e.g., 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂.
✅ Answer: A chemical reaction in which heat is produced (released) is called an exothermic reaction; e.g., combustion of tungsten in a bulb.
Explain the Statements with Reasons
✅ Answer:
Atoms are not destroyed but merely rearranged; the number and mass of atoms in reactants remain the same in products, so mass remains constant.
✅ Answer:
Energy (heat) is required to break the bonds in a compound, so decomposition is usually endothermic.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
According to the law of conservation of mass, mass does not change in a reaction, so balancing the equation is necessary. Balancing ensures equal numbers of atoms on both sides and gives the correct proportion of the reaction.
✅ Answer:
Combination: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. Decomposition: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. Displacement: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu. Double displacement: AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃. Each involves exchange of components and formation of new substances.
✅ Answer:
According to Lavoisier's law, 'In a chemical reaction, the mass of substances is neither created nor destroyed; the mass of reactants = the mass of products.' Atoms are merely rearranged, not destroyed, hence mass remains constant.
