✅ Answer: b) Heat production (heating)

When electric current flows through a conductor, the conductor becomes hot; this is called Joule heating. H = I²Rt.

  • a) Voltmeter

  • a) By static electric charge

✅ Answer: b) By a current-carrying coil

When electric current flows through a wire wound around an iron or soft iron core, it becomes an electromagnet.

  • a) Voltage
  • b) Direction of electromagnetic force
  • c) Mass
  • d) Temperature
✅ Answer: b) Direction of electromagnetic force

The direction of force acting on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field is given by Fleming's left-hand rule.

  • a) Measuring current
  • b) Measuring potential difference
  • c) Measuring resistance
  • d) Frequency
✅ Answer: b) Measuring potential difference

A voltmeter measures potential difference (voltage) between two points and is connected in parallel in the circuit.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (3) Transformer – Frequency

A transformer is used to increase or decrease voltage (voltage), not frequency.

✅ Wrong Pair: (2) Fleming right – Voltage

Fleming's right-hand rule gives the direction of induced current (electromagnetic induction), not voltage.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: When electric current is passed through a coil of wire wound around a soft iron core, it becomes a magnet; the magnetism disappears when the current is switched off; this is called an electromagnet.
✅ Answer: When electric current flows through a conductor, heat is produced in the conductor; H = I²Rt. This is used in bulbs, irons, and kettles.
✅ Answer: The phenomenon of induced current being produced in a conductor due to a changing magnetic field is called electromagnetic induction; this is the principle of a generator.
✅ Answer: Left-hand rule gives the direction of magnetic force on a current-carrying conductor; right-hand rule gives the direction of induced current.
Explain the Statements with Reasons
✅ Answer:
Free electrons in the conductor collide with the molecules of the conductor, converting kinetic energy into heat, making the wire hot (Joule heating).
✅ Answer:
In a generator, a coil is rotated in a changing magnetic field, producing induced current; this is the principle of electromagnetic induction.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
When a current-carrying wire wound around a soft iron core becomes a magnet, it is called an electromagnet. Applications: electric bell, motors, lifts, MRI machines, scrap separation, railways, etc.
✅ Answer:
Left hand: forefinger = current, middle finger = magnetic field, thumb = direction of force. Right hand: forefinger = changing magnetic field, thumb = direction of induced current. Left for force, right for induced current.
✅ Answer:
When electric current flows through a conductor, heat is produced (H = I²Rt). Applications: electric bulb, iron, kettle, heater, toaster. Adverse effects include energy loss in wiring and accidents.
