In the modern periodic table, horizontal rows are called 'Periods' and vertical columns are called 'Groups'.

  • a) Atomic mass
  • b) Outer shell electrons

The modern periodic table is based on atomic number; properties repeat as atomic number increases.

  • a) Group 1 (IA)
  • b) Group 2 (IIA)

  • d) Group 18 (VIIIA)
✅ Answer: a) Group 1 (IA)

Alkali metals (Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Fr) are in Group 1 (IA) and are highly reactive.

  • a) Group 1
  • b) Group 2
  • c) Group 17
  • d) Group 18
✅ Answer: d) Group 18

Noble gases (He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn) are in Group 18; their outer shell is completely filled, making them inert.

  • a) Atomic mass
  • b) Atomic number
  • c) Mass number
  • d) Electron configuration
✅ Answer: a) Atomic mass

Mendeleev arranged elements in increasing order of atomic mass; later Moseley gave the modern table based on atomic number.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Group – Horizontal rows

Groups are vertical columns and Periods are horizontal rows; therefore this pair is wrong.

✅ Wrong Pair: (3) He – Alkali metal

He (Helium) is a noble gas, not an alkali metal; alkali metals are Na, K, etc. in Group 1.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: The arrangement of elements in increasing order of their atomic number such that elements with similar properties fall in the same vertical column (group) is called the periodic table.
✅ Answer: The vertical column in the periodic table is called a group. Elements in the same group have similar outer shell electron configurations, hence similar properties.
✅ Answer: He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn in Group 18 are called noble gases. Their outer shell is completely filled, so they are inert (non-reactive).
Explain the Statements with Reasons
✅ Answer:
As atomic number increases, the number and configuration of outer shell electrons repeat regularly, hence periodicity of properties is observed.
✅ Answer:
Noble gases have a completely filled outer electron shell (octet); therefore they do not readily form bonds with other elements.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Mendeleev arranged elements in increasing order of atomic mass and discovered periodicity of properties; but there were some misplaced elements and gaps. Moseley proved that atomic number is the fundamental property and gave the modern periodic table based on atomic number, which is more accurate.
✅ Answer:
The modern periodic table has 7 periods (horizontal rows) and 18 groups (vertical columns). From left to right, the order is metals → metalloids → non-metals. This table helps understand properties, valency, atomic size, and reactivity of elements.
✅ Answer:
Alkali metals (Group 1): shiny, soft, conductors of heat and electricity, highly reactive, react vigorously with water. Noble gases (Group 18): gaseous state, inert, completely filled outer shell, non-reactive.
