Multiple Choice Questions
  • a) Change in speed of light
  • b) Change in color of light
  • c) Reflection
  • d) Curvature
✅ Answer: a) Change in speed of light

When light passes from one medium to another, its speed changes, causing the ray to change direction; this is called refraction.

  • a) sin r / sin i
  • b) sin i / sin r
  • c) i × r
  • d) i + r
✅ Answer: b) sin i / sin r

According to Snell's law, refractive index n = sin i / sin r, where i is the angle of incidence and r is the angle of refraction.

  • a) 1.0
  • b) 1.33
  • c) 1.5
  • d) 2.42
✅ Answer: b) 1.33

The refractive index of water is 1.33, glass is 1.5, and diamond is 2.42.

  • a) Rarer→Denser medium
  • b) Denser→Rarer and angle greater than critical angle
  • c) Perpendicular incidence
  • d) Without reflection
✅ Answer: b) Denser→Rarer and angle greater than critical angle

When light goes from a denser to a rarer medium and the angle of incidence is greater than the critical angle, the light bends back; this is called total internal reflection.

  • a) 1.0
  • b) 1.33
  • c) 1.5
  • d) 2.0
✅ Answer: a) 1.0

Air (vacuum) has a refractive index of 1; all other substances have a refractive index greater than 1.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (4) Air – 2.42

Air has a refractive index of 1; 2.42 is the refractive index of diamond.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Refraction is the phenomenon of a light ray bending when it changes medium. Snell's law: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r; i.e., for two media, sin i / sin r is constant (refractive index).
✅ Answer:
When going from a denser to a rarer medium, if the angle of incidence is greater than the critical angle, light is reflected back (total internal reflection). Examples: mirage, optical fibers, diamond sparkle.
✅ Answer:
Due to refraction: (1) Objects in water appear closer, (2) A pencil appears bent in water, (3) Mirage, (4) Image formation by the lens in the eye, (5) Rainbow.
