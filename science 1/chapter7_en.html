Multiple Choice Questions
  • a) Thinner in middle, always virtual image
  • b) Thicker in middle, real/virtual image
  • c) Parallel lens
  • d) Only virtual image
✅ Answer: b) Thicker in middle, real/virtual image

A convex (converging) lens is thicker in the middle and produces either a real or virtual image depending on the object's position.

  • a) Thicker in middle
  • b) Always real image
  • c) Thinner in middle, always virtual and diminished image
  • d) Converging
✅ Answer: c) Thinner in middle, always virtual and diminished image

A concave (diverging) lens is thinner in the middle and always produces a virtual, erect, and diminished image.

  • a) Concave
  • b) Convex
  • c) Parallel
  • d) Prism
✅ Answer: b) Convex

A convex lens is used in the eye and camera; it forms a real image on the retina/film.

  • a) Concave
  • b) Convex
  • c) Prism
  • d) Bifocal
✅ Answer: b) Convex

In cataract surgery, the clouded natural lens is removed and a convex (converging) artificial lens is implanted.

  • a) Convex
  • b) Concave
  • c) Parallel
  • d) Prism
✅ Answer: b) Concave

In myopia, distant objects are clear but nearby objects are not; a concave (diverging) lens is used.

Identify the Wrong Pair
✅ Wrong Pair: (3) Cataract – Concave lens

A convex (converging) artificial lens is used for cataract, not a concave lens.

Write a Note
✅ Answer: A lens that is thicker in the middle and thinner at the edges is called a convex (converging) lens. It converges incident rays to a single point (focus).
✅ Answer: A lens that is thinner in the middle and thicker at the edges is called a concave (diverging) lens. It diverges rays and always gives a virtual image.
✅ Answer: The distance from the center of a lens to its focal point is called the focal length; the focal length of a convex lens is positive and that of a concave lens is negative.
Explain the Statements with Reasons
✅ Answer:
A convex lens forms a real, inverted image on the retina; the brain corrects it to be upright, so we see objects correctly.
✅ Answer:
In myopia, the image is formed in front of the retina; a concave lens diverges the rays, bringing the image onto the retina.

Detailed Answers
✅ Answer:
Convex: thicker in middle, converging, real/virtual image, positive focal length. Concave: thinner in middle, diverging, always virtual/diminished image, negative focal length. Convex used in eye/camera/cataract; concave used in myopia spectacles.
✅ Answer:
The eye has a convex lens and a retina. The lens forms a real, inverted image on the retina; ciliary muscles change the curvature of the lens to control focal length (accommodation). The brain corrects the image to be upright.
✅ Answer:
Camera: convex lens forms image on film. Microscope: magnifies small objects (convex). Telescope: brings distant objects closer (convex). Spectacles: convex for hypermetropia, concave for myopia.
